<h1 align="center">Hurukan</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>The superintelligent agentic operating system for the sovereign enterprise.</strong></p>

One operating system for the whole company instead of ERP, CRM, WMS and the spreadsheets between them. Agents build and run it on a self-developing model of your business, people decide what matters, and all of it runs on infrastructure you choose. Hurukan is built on sovereign open source.

## What it is

- **One data model, one login.** Ten modules share one directory of companies, people, items and documents, so a customer in marketing is the same customer in commerce, service and finance.
- **Agents propose, people decide.** Every agent works inside written limits, proposals land in one approval inbox, and every action is logged with who, what and why.
- **A model of the company that grows with it.** Objects, relations and rules are written down and versioned; agents propose changes, people approve them.
- **Sovereign by design.** Runs on infrastructure you choose, with connectors that bring in what you already hold instead of syncing copies between products.
